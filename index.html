Questions we hear most often

How long does onboarding take? +

Most teams get up and running within less than an hour. Our intuitive visual builder and pre-built templates allow immediate workflow setup without requiring complex code deployment.

Can I migrate from my current platform? +

Yes, Luminary offers automated migration scripts and native import tools to effortlessly transition your existing pipelines, data models, and workflow configurations.

What happens when I exceed my plan limits? +

We will notify you when you reach 80% and 100% of your usage limits. Your workflows will continue to run smoothly, and you will have a graceful grace period to upgrade your plan.

Is my data encrypted? +

All data is encrypted both in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption standards. We strictly follow enterprise SOC2 Type II compliance controls.

Do you offer a free trial? +

Yes! We offer a full-featured 14-day free trial on all plans without requiring any credit card details up front.
